# Self-Service Portal Guide

For Employees (`USER`) and Managers (`SUPERVISOR`). The portal is at `/selfservice/`; these users have no console access.

## Employees
- **New Helpdesk Ticket:** choose a category and subcategory, describe the issue, attach files. IT staff triage it.
- **New Change Request:** request an IT change; it enters the normal change-management workflow.
- **My Requests:** status of everything you submitted.
- **Policies:** read published policies and acknowledge them (your acknowledgement is recorded).
- **Security Awareness Training:** complete the training assigned through active campaigns.
- Notifications appear in the portal when your requests change state.

## Managers
Managers can do everything an Employee can, plus:
- **My Team:** provision, edit, suspend or reactivate the Employees who report to you, reset their passwords or set a recovery passphrase.
- **Team Requests:** every ticket and change request raised by anyone in your reporting chain, at any status.
- **Approvals:** approve or reject pending tickets and change requests. Only approved items reach IT.
- Managers sit in a chain of up to five levels (Manager-1 to Manager-5). A higher-level Manager can see and approve for their whole downline.

## Turning on Manager approval
An Admin enables **Manager approval** in Platform and Access, Business Settings. From then on a request stays hidden from IT and GRC until a Manager approves it. Any Manager above the requester in the chain (Manager-1 up to Manager-5) can approve or reject, and a rejection records a reason. Register the chain under Platform and Access, Managers.

## Getting an account
An Admin, or a Manager for their own team, provisions the account and passes on the temporary password. There is no email delivery.
