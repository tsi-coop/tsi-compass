Business Settings

Deployment-wide configuration for this business. These settings apply across all users and branches.

Require Manager Approval

When enabled, a helpdesk ticket or change request submitted by an Employee user is held for their assigned Manager to approve or reject before IT/GRC staff can see it. Employees with no assigned Manager are blocked from submitting until one is assigned. Useful for businesses with a large branch footprint and lean IT staff who want line managers to triage requests first.

Only an Administrator can change business settings.
