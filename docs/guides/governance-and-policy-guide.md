# Governance and Policy Guide

For GRC Officers and Admins (`GRC_OFFICER` write, `IT_STAFF` no access).

## Committees
**Governance and Policy, Committees** holds the meeting calendar for your governance bodies. Three are seeded (IT Strategy Committee, IT Steering Committee, Risk Board); add your own. For each meeting you can set the date, venue and agenda, mark it Scheduled, Tentative, Conducted or Cancelled, **log minutes**, and track **action items** with an owner and status (single or bulk add).

## Policies and Documents
A register of policies, standards, guidelines and SOPs.
1. **Create** a document with a type, version, framework tags (for example ISO 27001 A.5.1, DPDP Sec 8), review date and body text. Optionally upload the file; it is stored and downloadable.
2. Move it through the lifecycle: **Draft, Under review, Approved, Published, Archived**. Approval records who approved.
3. **Publish** to make it visible to employees in the self-service portal, where they must acknowledge it.
4. Keep the review date current; policies are the natural evidence for controls that ask for a reviewed policy.

Documents can be arranged under a parent policy (policy, standard, SOP).

## Attestations
Employees acknowledge published policies from the [Self-Service Portal](self-service-portal-guide.md), and each acknowledgement is stored against the policy. (There is no separate attestation page in the console yet.)

## Tips
- Tag policies with the frameworks they support so the Controls module and auditors can find them.
- Record committee minutes and action items in the same system, so decisions and follow-up are one trail.
- Everything you change here is written to the System Audit Trail.
